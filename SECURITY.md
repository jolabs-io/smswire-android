# Security

Please don't open a public issue for a security problem. Email **support@smswire.app** with "Security" in the subject, what you found, and how to reproduce it. We answer within 3 working days and tell you when a fix ships.

In scope: this app, its pairing and its connection to the server, and anything that lets SMS or message data leave a phone other than as [the README](README.md#what-the-app-does-with-your-phone) describes. Problems in the hosted service (smswire.app, api.smswire.app) are welcome at the same address.
